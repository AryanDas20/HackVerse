PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Draft notice for the HackVerse service. The site owner must review and complete this notice before public commercial launch.

Information we store

HackVerse may store account identifiers and sign-in details through Supabase Auth; profile information you choose to provide; messages and group metadata; project and saved-opportunity records; and uploaded chat attachments or profile images stored using Supabase.

Profile visibility

Fields marked public may be visible to signed-in community members. Email and phone visibility are controlled separately in your profile settings. Do not publish information you do not want other members to see.

Messages and files

Chat attachments are intended to be stored in a private bucket and served using short-lived signed links after membership checks. Messages are stored in the HackVerse database and are not currently end-to-end encrypted. Do not use HackVerse to exchange highly sensitive information.

AI features

HackVerse AI and AI Wish send the message you submit and limited account or page context to the configured Agent37 service to generate responses. AI Wish action plans require your approval before supported changes are applied. Do not send passwords, secret keys, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Retention and deletion

Some records may remain in backups or security logs for a limited period. The current starter release does not provide a complete self-service account deletion and data-erasure workflow. Before launch, the owner must add a verified deletion-request channel, define retention periods, and document processor locations and contractual terms.
